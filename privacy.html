Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the mobile game THE HOUSE: Escape Room (the “Game”) published by Sky C&I.

1. Summary

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The Game works without an account and without an internet connection.

2. Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings (volume, language, look sensitivity) and hint tokens are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the Game deletes this data.

3. No accounts, ads or tracking

The Game has no sign-in, shows no advertising, and contains no analytics or tracking tools. We do not access your advertising ID, location, contacts, camera or microphone.

4. Purchases

Any in-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive your payment details.

5. Crash reports

If you have allowed it in your device settings, Google or Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. They contain no personal information and are used only to fix bugs.

6. Age

The Game contains simulated gambling and is intended for players aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if advertising is added), we will update this page and the date above.
